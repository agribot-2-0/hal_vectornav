Original source copyright and license headers and existing license files have been retained. Such file-specific terms continue to apply to the affected material. Dependencies obtained separately remain subject to their own licenses.

## Project-wide dependencies

The Agribot system uses ROS and third-party packages. In particular, rviz_visual_tools and open_street_map retain their BSD source notices; gazebo2rviz, pysdf and livox_ros_driver retain their MIT declarations/notices; Livox-SDK and imu_pipeline are external submodules. Their licensing is not converted to Apache-2.0 by this distribution. Third-party dependencies declared by Android/Gradle, ROS or vendor project exports retain their respective terms.

Vendor redistributable rights were not established for the copied Advantech/video installers and binary libraries, VectorNav SDK payload, or bundled PlantUML JAR. Those payloads have been omitted from this distribution. See VENDOR_DEPENDENCIES.md where applicable.
